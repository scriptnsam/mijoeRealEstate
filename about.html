Our Story

MIJOE REAL ESTATE AND AGENCY was officially established in 2022 by Joshua Timilehin, with a clear vision to revolutionize the way real estate transactions are conducted in Nigeria and beyond. Since our inception, we have remained committed to delivering reliable, transparent, and client-focused real estate solutions.

Over the years, we’ve helped thousands of individuals, investors, and businesses find their ideal properties—whether for residential, commercial, or investment purposes. Our approach is simple: build trust, provide expert guidance, and exceed expectations.

With office locations in Ado-Ekiti, Akure, Ibadan, Lagos and Abuja, we are strategically positioned to serve both urban and emerging markets. Whether you're buying, selling, renting, or investing, MIJOE REAL ESTATE AND AGENCY is your trusted partner in real estate success.

Our Mission

At MIJOE REAL ESTATE AND AGENCY, our mission is to provide seamless, reliable, and value-driven real estate solutions. We are committed to helping every client make informed decisions and achieve lasting satisfaction with their investments.

We believe in the power of transparency, trust, and quality service, ensuring that each interaction reflects our dedication to integrity and excellence.
